ORD, OTE, YTD and SG: The Payslip Abbreviations Australians Get Wrong

By J. Walker · November 2026

Your first Australian payslip can look like a wall of codes. Employers must include certain details by law, but most payroll systems squeeze them into two- and three-letter abbreviations that nobody explains.

Below are the codes that cause the most confusion, with a worked example. If you want every line of a payslip broken down with screenshots, this guide to payslip abbreviations explained goes through a full sample payslip line by line.

The earnings codes

  • ORD (Ordinary hours): pay for your normal rostered hours at your base rate. On a 38-hour week, this is usually 38 hours.
  • OT 1.5 / OT 2.0: overtime at time-and-a-half or double time. These rates come from your award or agreement.
  • ALLOW: allowances such as tool, travel or meal allowances. Some are taxed, some are not, so check how they are labelled.
  • AL / PL / LSL: annual leave, personal (sick or carer's) leave and long service leave taken in this period.
  • LL (Leave loading): usually an extra 17.5% on annual leave pay, if your award or contract includes it.

The tax and super codes

  • PAYG: the income tax your employer withholds and sends to the ATO each pay period.
  • SG (Superannuation Guarantee): the compulsory super your employer pays on top of your wage, 12% of ordinary time earnings from 1 July 2025.
  • OTE (Ordinary Time Earnings): the amount super is calculated on. It generally includes ORD and most allowances, but not overtime.
  • SS / SAL SAC: salary sacrifice, money you choose to send to super (or a packaged benefit) before tax.
  • YTD (Year to date): running totals since 1 July. Compare YTD gross against your income statement in myGov at tax time.

A quick example

Sam works 38 hours in a week at $35 per hour plus 4 hours overtime at time-and-a-half:

  • ORD: 38 x $35 = $1,330
  • OT 1.5: 4 x $52.50 = $210
  • Gross: $1,540
  • SG at 12% is worked out on the $1,330 OTE, not on the overtime: $159.60

If the SG line is calculated on your full gross including overtime, that is generous; if it is missing or lower than 12% of OTE, ask payroll.

Three lines to check every pay day

  1. Hours: ORD and overtime hours match your roster or timesheet.
  2. Super: SG is at least 12% of OTE and appears every pay (super must be paid at least quarterly, and from 1 July 2026 with each pay under Payday Super).
  3. YTD: the running totals keep growing in a straight line. A sudden drop usually means a payroll correction worth asking about.
